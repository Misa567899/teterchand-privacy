Privacy Policy

TeterChand — USDT Price Extension

Last updated: October 29, 2026

1. Overview

TeterChand is a Chrome extension that displays the USDT market price using publicly available market data from Wallex.

2. Information We Collect

The extension does not require an account and is not designed to collect personal information, browsing history, passwords, or website content. It does not include advertising or analytics tracking.

The extension sends requests to the Wallex API to retrieve market prices. Wallex may receive standard technical information associated with those requests, such as an IP address, according to its own services and policies.

3. Local Storage

The extension may store the latest retrieved price and its update timestamp locally in your browser using Chrome's storage facilities. This allows the last available price to remain visible when a refresh fails. This information is not sent to the extension developer.

4. Third-Party Service

Market data is requested from Wallex. The availability and accuracy of that data are subject to Wallex's service. Please review Wallex's website for information about its services and policies.

5. Data Security and Retention

Cached price information remains in your browser until it is replaced or removed. You can remove locally stored extension data by removing the extension or clearing its stored data through Chrome.

6. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated if the extension's functionality or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
